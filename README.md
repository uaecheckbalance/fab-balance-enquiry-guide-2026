# FAB Balance Enquiry Guide for UAE Customers

By Daniel Collins. This guide explains how to check a First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB) account balance in the UAE, using the methods most customers use every day. For a clear starting point, use this [balance enquiry](https://uaecheckbalance.com/) overview before you pick a method below.

## Before you start

Keep your registered mobile number active, because FAB uses it for alerts and verification. Know which account you want to check, especially if you hold a current account and a savings account. Check the balance at a quiet moment, so you can read the figures carefully and spot any pending transaction.

## Method 1: Online banking

Sign in to FAB online banking with your usual credentials. Open the accounts section and select the account you want to review. The dashboard normally shows the available balance and the current balance. The available balance is the amount you can use now. The current balance can include pending items that have not cleared yet. If you need detail, open recent transactions and compare the latest entries with your own receipts.

## Method 2: Mobile app

Open the FAB mobile app and complete the sign in step, such as a PIN or biometric check. Your accounts should appear on the home screen. Tap the account to see its balance and recent activity. Pull down to refresh if the screen looks out of date. If the app asks for an update, install it first, because an old version can show stale data.

## Method 3: SMS and alerts

If you have SMS alerts turned on, FAB sends a message after many transactions, and that message usually states the updated balance. Keep those messages for a short period, so you can compare them with the app or online banking. If you change your mobile number, update it with the bank first, or alerts will go to the old number. Do not reply to a balance message with your PIN, password, or one time code.

## Method 4: ATM

Insert your FAB card at an ATM and enter your PIN. Choose the balance inquiry option, then select the account type. The screen will show the balance, and you can print a receipt or a short mini statement if you want a paper record. Take your card before you leave, and check that the receipt matches the account you selected.

## Reading the result correctly

A lower available balance than expected often means a card payment, cheque, or transfer is still pending. A salary credit, refund, or transfer can also take time to move from pending to cleared. Compare the date and time of the last transaction with your own records. If a payment was made today, wait for it to clear before you judge the final figure.

## Troubleshooting

If the app shows an old balance, refresh the screen, check your internet connection, and sign out and back in. If online banking will not load, try another browser or device and clear the browser cache. If an ATM receipt looks wrong, keep the receipt and check the same account in the app. If the balance is still wrong after pending items clear, contact FAB through the official support channel in the app and keep the transaction date, amount, and receipt ready. Never share your PIN or one time code with anyone who calls or messages you about the problem.

## Quick checklist

1. Confirm the correct account is selected.
2. Check for pending transactions.
3. Refresh the app or online banking page.
4. Compare the result with your latest receipt or SMS alert.
5. Keep a record if you need to ask the bank about a difference.
